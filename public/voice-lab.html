Talk to Jinni

A demo of what this phone's browser can do, in English, Russian and Armenian. Nothing is saved.

Language

1 · You speak

Browser recognition is free and built into the phone. On iPhone it knows English and Russian, not Armenian. Cloud uses Whisper, works for all three on every phone, and needs the OpenAI key below (about half a cent per minute).

2 · Jinni speaks

SlowerFaster · 0.95

The browser voice is whatever this phone has installed: iPhone has natural English and Russian voices and no Armenian one. The OpenAI voice sounds like a person, reads all three languages, about a cent per answer, but it is one of six presets anyone can use. A Jinni voice of its own is made at ElevenLabs — designed from a description or recorded with an actor — and then used here by its voice id.

Cloud keys (optional)

Needed only for the cloud features. Paste them here on the phone to try; close the tab and they are gone.
